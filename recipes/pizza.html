pizza recipe

Description

Here is a simple recipe to make pizza at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of water
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of yeast
  • 1/2 cup of tomato sauce
  • 1 cup of mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup of pepperoni

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, water, olive oil, salt, sugar, and yeast in a bowl.
  2. Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  3. Let the dough rise for 1 hour.
  4. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees.
  5. Roll out the dough and place it on a baking sheet.
  6. Spread the tomato sauce on the dough.
  7. Sprinkle the cheese and pepperoni on top.
  8. Bake the pizza for 15 minutes.
  9. Enjoy your pizza!
