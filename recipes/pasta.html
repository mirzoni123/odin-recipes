pasta recipe

Description

Here is a simple recipe to make pasta at home.

Ingredients

  • pasta
  • water
  • salt
  • olive oil
  • tomato sauce
  • cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add pasta and salt to the boiling water.
  3. Let the pasta cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Drain the pasta and add olive oil.
  5. Stir in tomato sauce and cheese.
  6. Enjoy your pasta!
